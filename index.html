I am Changmin Lee, a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at KAIST, advised by Prof. Tae-Kyun Kim in the Computer Vision and Learning Lab (KCVL). I received my M.S. in Artificial Intelligence from the Kim Jaechul Graduate School of AI and my B.S. in Computer Science and Mathematical Sciences (double major) from KAIST.

My research lies at the intersection of 3D computer vision, computer graphics, and physics-based simulation. I am particularly interested in physics-aware scene understanding and reconstruction: recovering 3D humans, objects, and their interactions from visual observations in a way that is consistent with the underlying physical dynamics.
